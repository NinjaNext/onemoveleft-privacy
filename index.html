One Move Left Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026
App: One Move Left: Logic Puzzle (Android package com.ninjanext.onemoveleft)
Developer: NinjaNext — ninjanexttech.com
Contact: info@ninjanexttech.com

One Move Left is an offline logic puzzle game. It needs no account, shows no ads, and never connects to the internet. This policy explains the little the game stores on your device and confirms that NinjaNext collects none of it.

Information the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, One Move Left stores the following only on your device, in a local database (SQLite) inside the app’s private storage:

  • Game progress: which levels you’ve solved, the stars you earned, the number of attempts, and when each level was first solved
  • Achievements you’ve unlocked and simple statistics (total attempts, hints used, solve streaks and the date you last played, used for the day streak)
  • Settings: sound, vibration, reduced motion, colour glyphs and tutorial tips

None of this identifies you, and none of it leaves your device.

Information we collect

NinjaNext doesn’t collect, receive, sell or share any personal or usage information. One Move Left has no user accounts, no servers, no advertising, and no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. The game doesn’t use the internet at all, so it has no way to send anything anywhere. NinjaNext can’t see your progress.

Permissions

The game doesn’t request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or network. Haptic feedback (vibration) can be turned off in Settings.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Reset all progress in the game’s Settings
  • Clear the app’s storage in your device settings
  • Uninstall the game, which removes its stored data

Because your progress exists only on your device, deleting it is permanent unless your device’s own backup includes it. NinjaNext can’t recover it for you.

Children

One Move Left contains no violence, chat, purchases or ads, and is suitable for players of all ages. Links to the NinjaNext website and your email app are in Settings, and links to the Google Play pages of other NinjaNext apps appear in Settings, on the home screen and after you finish a level; they open only when you tap them, and the game itself never goes online. Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a feature is added, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any feature that would send data off your device would be optional, off by default, and would ask for your consent first.
